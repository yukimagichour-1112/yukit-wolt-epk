Psytrance · Night Full On / Twilight / Full On

YUKI.T is a Psytrance DJ based in Osaka, Japan. Known for a dynamic mixing style built around kick, bassline, energy flow and strong dancefloor control, he shapes each set as one continuous story. Label DJ for Patronus Records (CH) and Label Manager of Magichour Recordings. Curator of the FIRST IMPACT compilation series.

Night Full On、Twilight、Full Onを軸に、フロアの熱量を途切れさせないダイナミックなセットを展開する。

MO:DEM Festival 2024 / 2025 FIRST IMPACT curator

Techno / Psytech / Dark Minimal / Psytechno

WOLT is the Techno and Psytech alias. Hypnotic, deep and psychedelic grooves built for long nights, moving between raw Techno and psychedelic tension. Label DJ for AlpaKa MuziK (CA), resident of RHYTHM SHIFT, a weekly Techno night in Osaka, and curator of the Enchanted Tech Journey series.

HypnoticかつDeepなグルーヴで、TechnoとPsychedelicの間を行き来する名義。
